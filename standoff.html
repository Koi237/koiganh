How to play

Both players secretly build a move each turn, then everything resolves at once in the showdown. First to drop the other from 32 HP wins.

Each turn

Place one Action (required): Shoot (4 dmg), Shield (−75% damage taken) or Reload (refill to 2 bullets, draw 2 special cards — but +100% damage taken if shot). Optionally add an Element to a shot or shield, and a Mod to anything. You get 20 seconds; going AFK counts as a reload.

Elements

Fire > Ice > Water > Fire. Shooting into a shield: +50% if your element beats it, −50% if it loses. Hitting a reloading or shooting player with an elemental shot leaves a stain — it detonates for 4 dmg after two turns unless they play the counter element on a shot or shield first.

Mods

Vampiric — next bullet heals you 50% of its damage. Dark Steel — +25% dealt / −25% taken; ±100% if raw. Arcane Eater — strips the element off the enemy's shot this showdown, heal 3 if it eats one. Silver Hand — everyone takes ×2 final damage (stacks ×4). Rainbow — randomizes your element, raw damage (2–8), raw shield (−50…−125%) or reload draws (1–4). Love Lock — locks one random enemy special card next turn.

Cards

One reload and (from turn 2) one shield are re-dealt whenever you're out. Bullets cap at 2. Special cards come from reloading: 75% element, 25% mod.
