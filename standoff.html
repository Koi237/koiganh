Field manual

Both players secretly build an action, lock it, then reveal together. Reduce the rival from 32 health to zero. If both fall in the same showdown, the duel is a draw.

Build one turn

01 · ActionMandatory. Play Shoot, Shield, or Reload.
02 · ElementOptional on Shoot or Shield. Fire, Ice, or Water.
03 · ModifyOptional. Each mod changes the showdown in a different way.

You have 45 seconds. If time expires before you lock a turn, Reload is played automatically.

Core actions

  • Shoot deals 4 base damage and consumes one Shoot card.
  • Shield reduces incoming damage by 75%.
  • Reload makes you take 100% extra damage, refills your hand to two Shoot cards, and draws two special cards.
  • The first turn contains only Reload. From turn two onward, you always carry one Reload and one Shield at most.

Element cycle

FirebeatsIcebeatsWaterbeatsFire

When an elemental shot meets an elemental shield, advantage adds +50% damage and disadvantage adds −50%. Matching elements make no change.

An elemental shot that hits a player while they Shoot or Reload leaves a stain. It detonates for 4 damage after two more turns unless that player uses the element that beats the stain in a Shoot or Shield.

Modifier cards

  • Vampiric Bullet: Shoot only. Heal 50% of damage dealt.
  • Dark Steel: Shoot gains +25% damage or Shield receives −25%; both become 100% when the action is raw.
  • Arcane Eater: Arm a ward for the following turn. It strips the element from that turn’s enemy shot and heals 3 if an element is eaten.
  • Silver Hand: Every player takes ×2 final damage this showdown. Two copies stack to ×4.
  • Rainbow: Randomize a played element; or randomize raw Shoot damage, raw Shield reduction, or Reload draw count.
  • Love Lock: Lock one random rival special card next turn; if none exist, lock a Shoot.

Resolution order

Rainbow resolves first, stains can be cleansed, then an Arcane Eater ward armed on the prior turn removes an incoming element and makes that shot raw. Additive action, element, and Dark Steel bonuses are calculated before Silver Hand multiplies final damage. Shots and heals land simultaneously; due stains detonate, new stains are applied, Reload rewards are dealt, and Arcane Eater and Love Lock prepare their effects for the next turn.
